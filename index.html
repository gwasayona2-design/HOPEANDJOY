Welcome

Welcome to Hope and Joy Secondary School

Hope and Joy Secondary School is a leading co-educational secondary school located at Mbweni Beach, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We are committed to providing quality education that nurtures academic excellence, moral integrity, leadership, creativity and responsible citizenship.

Managed by the Sisters of the Congregation of the Virgin Mary, Mother of Orphans under the Archdiocese of Dar es Salaam, our school provides a safe, disciplined and caring environment where every student is encouraged to reach his or her full potential.

About Us

Rooted in Faith. Focused on Excellence.

Since 2013, the school has grown into a respected learning institution producing students who excel academically and demonstrate strong moral character.

01

Mission

To create and enable a better environment for instilling joy and nurturing well-educated citizens fully aware of their roles in society.

02

Vision

To be an important centre for efficiency, effectiveness, improvement and promotion of human values, understanding, competence and quality education, with emphasis on the future of children and orphans.

03

Values

Faith, respect, human dignity and national heritage guide our teaching, relationships and community life.
